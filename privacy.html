Privacy Policy

Effective: June 2, 2026

Split is a daily opinion game built by Cortex Studios. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

The short version: to use Split you create an account (with Apple Sign In or an email and password) and choose a first name and username. Your picks, comments, and profile are saved to your account so they follow you across devices. We use analytics to improve the app, and — if you allow it — a measurement tool to understand which ads brought people to Split. You can opt out of that ad tracking, and you can delete your account and data at any time.

Information We Collect

Account information. To use Split you sign in with Apple or with an email address and password. If you use Apple Sign In, we receive your email address (or Apple's private relay address) and, on first sign-in, your name if you choose to share it. If you sign up with email, we collect your email address. We use this to create and secure your account and to restore your data if you reinstall the app.

Profile information. You choose a first name and a username, and you may optionally add a profile photo, an avatar icon and color, and a country. These are shown to other users on your profile and next to your activity.

Your activity. Your picks, the comments and replies you post, who you follow, takes you star or submit, and any voice notes you send are stored on our servers and linked to your account. Comments, replies, and profile details are public within the app. Picks contribute to the anonymous crowd percentages everyone sees.

Device and usage information. We collect your device model and operating system version, app events (such as screens viewed and tutorial completion), a push notification token if you enable notifications, and an advertising identifier (described under "Advertising and Measurement" below) if you permit it.

Accounts and Authentication

Split uses accounts so your picks, profile, and history persist across reinstalls and devices. Authentication is handled with Apple Sign In and with email/password via our backend provider, Supabase. We do not store your password in readable form. Deleting your account (see "Your Rights") permanently removes your account and associated personal data.

Analytics

Split uses PostHog, a product analytics service, to understand how people use the app — which screens are visited, which questions get picks, and where users get stuck. This data is associated with an identifier for your account so we can understand behavior over time. It is used to improve the app, not to target advertising. PostHog's privacy policy is available at posthog.com/privacy.

Advertising and Measurement

Split runs advertising campaigns on TikTok to help new people discover the app. To measure whether those ads work, Split includes the TikTok Business SDK, which sends certain information — your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), an account identifier, and app events such as installs and tutorial completion — to TikTok. TikTok combines this with its own data to attribute installs to ads and optimize our campaigns. Under Apple's rules this is considered "tracking."

On iOS, we ask for your permission before any advertising identifier is used for this purpose, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, the advertising identifier is not used to track you, and you can change your choice at any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking. TikTok's privacy policy is available at tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Daily Questions

The daily questions are fetched from our servers (hosted by Supabase) when the app opens. The questions are sent to your device; this part of the process does not require sending your personal information.

Third-Party Services

Split uses the following third-party services:

Supabase provides our database and authentication — questions, comments, picks, profiles, voice notes, and account records. Supabase's privacy policy is available at supabase.com/privacy.

PostHog provides product analytics. Usage events are sent to PostHog for analysis. PostHog's privacy policy is available at posthog.com/privacy.

TikTok provides advertising measurement through the TikTok Business SDK, as described under "Advertising and Measurement." TikTok's privacy policy is available at tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy.

Apple provides the App Store, Apple Sign In, and push notification delivery. Apple's privacy practices are governed by apple.com/privacy.

Data Storage and Security

Your account and activity (account identifiers, profile, picks, comments, voice notes) are stored on our servers (Supabase) with encryption in transit and at rest. Some data — such as your picks and preferences — is also cached locally on your device. Because your data is tied to your account, it can be restored when you reinstall the app.

Children's Privacy

Split is intended for users aged 12 and older and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Advertising measurement (tracking) only occurs with explicit permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, and never if you decline. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us personal information, please contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data with third parties only as described in this policy: with Supabase to operate the app, with PostHog for analytics, and with TikTok for advertising measurement when you have permitted tracking.

Anonymous, aggregated results (such as "72% of users picked YES on this question") are displayed within the app as part of the core experience. This aggregated data cannot be used to identify individual users.

We may disclose information if required by law, such as in response to a valid court order or subpoena, and would notify affected users unless legally prohibited from doing so.

Your Rights

You can access and edit your profile information at any time in the app. You can delete your account — and the personal data associated with it — directly in the app under Settings, which permanently removes your account and personal data from our servers. You may also contact us at the address below to request access to or deletion of your data. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy as the app evolves. If we make material changes, we will notify you through the app or update the effective date at the top of this page, which always reflects the latest version.
